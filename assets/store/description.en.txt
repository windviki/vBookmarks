vBookmarks — your bookmarks, one keypress away.

Stop digging through Chrome's bookmark manager. vBookmarks puts your entire library in a fast, keyboard-friendly popup: click the toolbar icon (or press Ctrl+Shift+V), type a few letters, hit Enter, and you're there. It's the actively maintained successor of the classic Neat Bookmarks — free, lightweight, and polished since 2011.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Instant fuzzy search over titles, URLs and folders — in the popup, the side panel, or Chrome's address bar (type * then Space). Match highlighting included, CJK-friendly.
• Seven purpose-built views: the classic folder tree, search, tab groups, a staging workbench, visit statistics, dead-link cleanup and duplicate cleanup.
• A command palette (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K) for everything: open, move, sort, jump between views, switch themes — plus your own custom slash commands, synced across devices.

ADDING AND ORGANIZING, EFFORTLESSLY

• Save the current tab from the star button, Ctrl+D, the global Alt+Shift+S, or the right-click menu — always into the folder you chose.
• Capture a whole window of tabs as a bookmark folder with one command, and reopen it later as a named Chrome tab group.
• Organize at speed: drag and drop, multi-select batch actions, copy or move across folders, per-folder sorting, and edit dialogs that pre-fill everything for you.
• Every deletion is undoable — one tap on the toast brings it all back, so cleanup never feels risky.

A REAL KEYBOARD WORKFLOW

• Every view works without a mouse: arrows, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown, Enter to open, F2 to rename, Delete to remove, and type-ahead jumps in the tree.
• Alt+1…7 jumps straight to any view, Tab cycles the zones, and Esc unwinds menus, palette, selection and search — one clean layer at a time.
• Shortcuts everywhere: Ctrl+K / Cmd+K in the popup, Ctrl+Shift+K globally, Alt+Shift+B for the side panel, Alt+Shift+S for quick add.

THE STAGING WORKBENCH — A NEW WAY TO TRIAGE

• New bookmarks land in a staging area instead of disappearing into the tree: group them, star them, file them into folders in batches, or let them flow into the recently-added timeline.
• It turns "save now, organize someday" into a five-minute daily habit.

BEYOND STOCK CHROME

• Real separators — divider lines with custom colors and widths inside any folder, the feature Chrome's manager never had.
• Automatic favicon refresh recovers missing site icons (direct fetch, proxy, or provider fallbacks, with a local cache), so every row stays readable.
• Dead-link scan with an optional proxy re-check tells "site is down" from "blocked on your network"; duplicates merge with six keeper strategies.
• Sync-status indicators show which bookmarks live only on this device (Chrome 138+).
• Visit statistics stay 100% local: sort by count or recency, pause anytime, erase with one click.

MAKE IT YOURS

• Four crafted themes — Light, Dark, Ink, Paper — plus follow-system, zoom, custom CSS, and your own toolbar icon.
• Popup or side panel: the panel turns vBookmarks into a permanent workspace column beside your pages — and the foundation for everything coming next.
• Fully translated into 43 languages.

FREE AND PRIVATE

Free forever. No ads, no tracking, no accounts — your data never leaves your browser. Open source under the MIT license, maintained by a developer who still answers every issue.

Give it a week — your bookmarks will feel organized for the first time in years.
